- Repository: https://github.com/RubenM1990/APEX-UI
- Live Reference: https://apex-ui-xi.vercel.app/
- License: MIT License

### Adapted Components:
1. **ReasoningWeb** (`apex-reasoning-web.js`):
   - Pure SVG and requestAnimationFrame neural constellation graph.
   - Radial capability spoke layout, flowing particle light packets along active bezier connections, concentric orbital rings, and fanning PCB circuit traces.
2. **OrbStatusBar** (`apex-status-bar.js`):
   - SVG equalizer waveform, central glowing amber/cyan status ring, state indicator (STANDBY / PROCESSING / SPEAKING / LISTENING), and discoverability hint.
3. **ShaderBackground** (`apex-shader-background.js`):
   - WebGL plasma waves animated background adapted from community components on 21st.dev via APEX-UI (MIT-licensed).
