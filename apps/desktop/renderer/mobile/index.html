Pair this phone

On your PC, open Pandesh, then System → Connections → Phone. Scan the QR code there with this phone's camera, or type the code.

Can't connect?
  • Check that the PC is on and Pandesh is running.
  • Secure link (https, through Tailscale): turn Tailscale on in its app on this phone.
  • Wi-Fi link (http): this phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the PC, not a guest network, with no VPN on.
  • A code works once and for 10 minutes. Show a new one on the PC if it expired.

Can't reach your PC

Pandesh runs on your PC, and this phone could not reach it.

  • Is the PC on, with Pandesh running?
  • Secure link: is Tailscale turned on in its app on this phone?
  • Wi-Fi link: is this phone on the same Wi-Fi as the PC (not a guest network, no VPN)?
