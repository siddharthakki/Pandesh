# Pandesh Community Code of Conduct

We want Pandesh to be a respectful place for people learning, using, building and testing local AI.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful, inclusive, patient and constructive.
- Critique implementations and ideas rather than insulting people.
- Welcome beginner questions, accessibility feedback and different technical approaches.
- Respect contributors' privacy. Never pressure anyone to share files, accounts, credentials or identifying details.
- Do not harass, discriminate against, threaten or personally attack others.
- Do not post spam, unsolicited advertising, sexually explicit or abusive content.

## Project discussions and issues

Stay on topic. Report reproducible behavior, explain expected outcomes and share only redacted or synthetic examples. Disagreement and criticism are welcome when technically grounded and courteous. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content, close issues, and restrict participation to maintain a constructive community.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Do not publicly expose a victim's identity or private messages. Contact the maintainers using a non-sensitive request for a private reporting channel in GitHub, or use GitHub's available private reporting methods where appropriate. **No private moderator contact address has been designated yet.** This is a limitation the maintainers intend to resolve before community activity grows.

The maintainers will review reports based on available evidence, prioritizing safety and fairness. No response time or outcome is guaranteed.

This Code of Conduct covers the project's GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests and official discussion spaces. It does not confer authority over independent third-party sites.

See also [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [Security](SECURITY.md).
