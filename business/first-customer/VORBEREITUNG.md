# Preparation: two discovery conversations (INTERNAL DRAFT)

Run by the owner personally. No outreach by Pandesh. Only people you already know or who agreed. Use `GESPRAECHSLEITFADEN.md`, record in `ANTWORTEN.csv` right after each call.

## What we need to learn (nothing else)
1. Do they have a real problem? (They name it unprompted *and* tried something against it.)
2. Would they consider paying €290 once? (Reaction after you state the price and stay silent.)
3. What would stop them buying this week? (Their exact words.)

## Before each call
- Phone or printout of `sample-site/index.html`; show it only after question 5 and only if a real problem came up.
- Know your two permission questions (notes ok? contact ok?). Without a yes, store no name.
- Do not mention Pandesh, AI, or a finished offer. You are checking whether the service is worth offering.

## Right after each call (5 minutes)
- Fill one row. Quote the objection verbatim. Score need 0–3 honestly; a polite "sounds nice" is 1.
- Write one line: what surprised you.

## After both calls: decision rule
| Result | Next step |
|---|---|
| Both 0–1 need, no price reaction | Problem not real for this segment. Do not build anything; pick another segment or stop. |
| One or both 2–3 need, price "too much" | Ask what would make it a clear yes (already in guide). Do not discount yet; get more data. |
| One or both 2–3 need and €290 plausible | Talk to 3–5 more before any outreach, tooling or payment setup. Two people are not evidence of a market. |
